Commission Structure

Per the agency contracts: a producer's commission is a tiered % of their own new-business premium this folio, and the tier reached applies to the whole amount ($30K at 4.5% = $1,350). Standard producer tiers: 3% from $0 · 4.5% at $20K · 6% at $35K · 8% at $50K. Greg (agent) runs flat 10%. Carrier lines (set per deal on the card's Sold section): Bristol West pays 2 points below the tier rate and Kraft Lake / Life pays 50% of it — all premium still counts toward reaching the tier. Life bonus (+1% tier boost at 5 life apps, +2% at 10) and the $2,800 base are handled outside the board. Only the PIN changes these.
